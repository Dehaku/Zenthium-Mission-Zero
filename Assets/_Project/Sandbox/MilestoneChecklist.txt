<b>Milestone 7a: what to test in Sandbox_Repo</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, hero select top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene. The lost Valuables count is at the top center.
The Repo squad (GleebPeep, 2 Greys, a Tractor Drone, a Shield Bearer) patrols the north, all at rank B. Green discs
are crop circles (east and west). Citizens: downed (1 Valuable) and Stubborn north of you, Scared to the east.
A stash of 3 Valuables lies north-west. Labels show each entity's state and each citizen's kind.

<b>Crop circles and dragging</b>
1. At the start, a Grey walks to the downed citizen, drags it ("Looting", then "Objective") into the nearest crop
   circle, and the citizen vanishes for good. Its Valuable lies where it was downed.
2. The other Grey drags the Stubborn citizen (it walks slower while dragging it) into a crop circle.
3. Repo entities that pick up Valuables carry them into a crop circle, GleebPeep included: lost count goes up.
4. Walk into a crop circle carrying Valuables: you lose them all (lost count goes up). You aren't abducted.
5. Drag a citizen yourself into a crop circle: it's abducted. Come within range of a Grey dragging a citizen: it
   stops to shoot you, still holding the citizen, and carries on to the crop circle once you back off.

<b>Modules (in priority order)</b>
6. GleebPeep fights with Call for Backup (3-5 goons and 2 Champions appear around it, every 30 s), Repossess (one of
   your Gear slots is disabled for 5 s), Final Notice (Marked for 6 s), Tractor Beam (you're pulled toward it;
   its own goons aren't), then Tablet Slap (melee with knockback). MoreInfo shows the disabled slot.
7. Set GleebPeep's Difficulty Level to 0 (rank D) in the Inspector before Play: it only uses Tractor Beam and
   Tablet Slap. At 1 (rank C) it adds Repossess and Final Notice, but not Call for Backup.
8. The Tractor Drone pulls you (or a citizen) toward the nearest crop circle. The Shield Bearer can't be knocked
   back (Kick it), and raises its Energy Shield.
9. Greys are Cowards from the data: below 20% HP, 1 in 4 runs for the map edge. Backup at rank B can include a Tow
   Saucer, which does nothing yet (Milestone 7b).
