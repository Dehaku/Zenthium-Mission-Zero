<b>Milestone 6b: what to test in Sandbox_AI</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, hero select top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene. The lost Valuables count is at the top center.
Squad A (3 melee goons and their Leader, GleebPeep) patrols north-east. Squad B (2 Energy Blaster goons and a
Cannons goon) patrols north, beside a stash of 3 Valuables. A second stash of 4 lies east of your start.
Citizens: Scared (2 Valuables) south, downed (1 Valuable) west of your start, Stubborn by the turret (south-west).
Labels show each goon's state (with pickup %) and each citizen's kind.

<b>Looting</b>
1. At the start, Squad B walks to its stash, and each goon picks up a bag (about 5 s, "Looting 50%"). Hit a
   looter: its pickup starts over.
2. "Ranged Goon 2 (loot off the map)" carries its bag to the north edge and vanishes: lost count +1. The others
   carry theirs to the Leader ("Objective"), hand them over (the Leader's bag count goes up), then patrol again.
3. Fight by the east stash: melee goons loot it while you're out of their punch reach and turn on you when you
   come close. Ranged goons keep shooting while you're within their range.
4. Defeat the Leader, then let a goon pick up a bag: with no Leader standing, it carries it off the map.

<b>Cowardice</b>
5. Get a goon (not the Leader or the locked-in goon) below 20% HP. About 1 time in 4 its label shows "(panic)":
   it ignores you, runs for the nearest map edge and vanishes (what it carried counts as lost). It rolls once.

<b>Citizens</b>
6. Lead goons toward the Scared citizen: when it sees one within 20 m, it drops its 2 Valuables (once) and runs
   straight away ("Scared (fleeing)"). It doesn't react to goons it can't see.
7. The downed citizen lies with its Valuable beside it. Rush Hour's First Aid Spray (Gear) next to it gets it up
   as "Scared".
8. Drag grabs citizens standing or down. Dragging the Stubborn citizen slows you by 20%; it never walks on its own.
9. Rush Hour has Charming. Walk near the Stubborn citizen: one 50% roll to turn it Scared (Reset scene to roll
   again). Each goon you get near rolls once at 5%: a converted goon follows you as an ally and hands you its
   Valuables.
