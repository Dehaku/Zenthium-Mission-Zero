<b>Milestone 9a: what to test in the Mission scene</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, launcher top right.)
Launch starts a mission; Abandon goes back to the launcher. "Random" rolls a choice the way a real mission does.
The Console logs each mission's setup and what was spawned. Missions can't be won or lost until 9b.

<b>Launching</b>
1. Pick a starting hero, leave everything else on Random and rank D, and Launch: you're that hero, on a Small map,
   in an exit zone (blue square) at the map's edge. The map is black around what you can see.
2. Abandon, then Launch again with the same seed: the same mission, map and spawns. New seed gives a new one.
3. The launcher remembers your choices after Abandon. Medium or Large under Size overrides the rank's size.

<b>The map by rank</b>
4. Rank D has 4 exit zones, rank C 3, rank A 2, rank S 1, and they keep away from the mission's location.
5. A Countdown mission's key building has every door locked: walking up doesn't open them; break them.
   At rank D they break like Wood or Glass; at rank C some take as long as Stone.

<b>What a mission fills</b>
6. Greys stand in squads of 3-5 round the map and patrol between nearby spots. A rank D Small map has 30 Greys
   and no Champions; a rank D Medium map has 60 points, and 10% of it buys one Champion.
7. Citizens (pale yellow) stand in some open spots, mostly Scared, some down (Incapacitated) or Stubborn.
   Now and then one carries a Valuable (money bag icon).
8. Valuables lie in buildings: a bank holds 20-40, a store 5-10, most others 1.
9. Rescue: the key building holds 6 citizens on a Small map (10 Medium), and squads stand near citizens.
   Countdown: about half the squads stand round the key building. Arson Riot: squads spread out across the map.
   Invasion: only a quarter of the force is there at the start.
10. Leader "yes": 30 s in, GleebPeep (purple) appears at a spot at least 20 m from you.
