<b>Milestone 5a: what to test</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, hero select top right.)
Labels show every wall's, door's and prop's HP. Rush Hour: Strength 20, so Kick deals 50. Dummies: 100 HP, 5 Armor.
The debug panel (top left) shows and changes <b>you</b>: use it to raise your Strength.

<b>Walls, doors and windows</b>
1. Sample walls (bottom row): Kick each. Glass breaks at once (10 HP); Wood loses 50 per Kick (100 HP); Stone 300,
   Metal 600, Crystalline 1000. A broken wall vanishes: walk through the gap.
2. Wall impact: Kick a dummy into a wall from under 4 m. The wall takes 1000 (Heavy 100 from your Strength, x10)
   and breaks, even Crystalline; the dummy takes 20 (25 minus its 5 Armor).
3. Wood house (top right): its south door opens while you stand in it and closes when you step away.
4. Stone shop (top left, L-shaped): its Metal door is locked and stays shut. Kick a dummy into it: it breaks.
5. Windows (west side of each building): Kick one; it breaks (Glass, 10 HP).

<b>Props</b> (the row above the sample walls)
6. Drag (F) the bench. The dumpster needs Strength 50 and the tree 250: Drag skips them until you set your
   Strength that high on the debug panel.
7. Kick while dragging: the prop flies 4 m along your aim and hits the first dummy, wall or prop for your Kick
   times its factor (bench x1, dumpster x2, tree x3), and takes the same. The bench always breaks.
   A thrown prop hitting a wall, door or window deals it x2. Thrown bench (50) into a Wood wall: 100, it breaks.
8. Major Overclock (hero select), Power 3, Power Throw: throws everything you drag 15 m; props hit as in 7.
9. Car: each Kick deals 15 (50 minus 20 Armor, halved). At 0 HP a red ring 30 m wide appears; 1 s later it
   explodes for 500 to everything inside, you included, and to walls and props. Stand back. The motorcycle,
   7 m away, breaks and explodes too.

<b>Breaching Charge and the turret</b>
10. Major Overclock, Gear 3, Breaching Charge next to the Stone shop: walls, doors and windows in the 15 m
    blast take 10x its damage (Demolition) and all break.
11. Heavy Turret (bottom right corner): Neutral, it fires Heavy Gun at the dummy next to it every 2 s
    (45 per hit). Kick it: it never moves. Defeat it (5 Kicks), then Drag (F) next to it: it can't be dragged.

<b>NavMesh</b> (Scene view, with AI Navigation's Show NavMesh on)
12. Building walls and windows cut holes in the NavMesh; doors and broken walls don't.
