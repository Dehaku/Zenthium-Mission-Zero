<b>Milestone 5b: what to test</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, hero select top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene without leaving Play mode.
Rush Hour: Strength 20 (Kick 50), Will 10. Dummies: 100 HP, 5 Armor. Building labels show their fire state.

<b>Fire</b> (hero select: Test module <b>Fire Wand</b>, Apply; it replaces Power 4)
1. Fire Wand a Wood house wall: the house turns orange and its label counts "Burning N s".
2. Every 5 s everyone inside gets Burn (stand inside: you lose 2 HP every 0.5 s, with no gap; it ignores
   Armor). Every 10 s one random wall, window or door breaks.
3. After 60 s it's burned out: what's left of it turns black, and the 3 bags inside melt: "Valuables lost: 3"
   at the top of the screen.
4. Spreading is on: while one building burns, every 15 s the other (19 m away) has a 25% chance to catch fire.
5. Props: Fire Wand the dumpster (50 HP): the shot deals 15, then its label says Burning and it loses 2 HP
   every 0.5 s until it breaks. Burn on props lasts 50 s (10x).
6. Fire Wand the car: Burn ignores its Armor, so it reaches 0 in about 25 s and explodes; the blast sets the
   motorcycle on fire too.
7. Scene view (Show NavMesh): a burning building's floor is cut out of the NavMesh, and comes back when it's
   out or burned out.

<b>Putting fires out</b> (Test module <b>Water Cannon</b>; it replaces Gear 4 and costs 5 Ammo)
8. Water Cannon a wall of a burning building: it goes out (its colors come back), and can burn again later
   from a fresh 60 s.
9. Water Cannon a burning prop: Burning goes from its label. Push a dummy into a burning building (Water
   Cannon knocks back 15 m), wait for Burn on its label, then Water Cannon it: the Burn ends.

<b>Valuables</b>
10. Drag (F) next to a bag (two by your start): it's picked up at once. A "$1" shows under you; each bag adds
    -5% speed (label: CarryingTreasure), up to -50% at 10.
11. Kick with bags and no dragged prop: they all fly about 4 m along your aim, scattering. One that hits a
    dummy deals 45 (Kick 50 minus 5 Armor). They land as bags you can pick up again.
12. The dummy marked "carries 3 Valuables" shows "$3". Defeat it: "KA-CHING!", and 3 bags drop around it.
13. Drag a prop and carry bags: the first Kick throws the prop, the next launches the bags.
