<b>Milestone 8b: what to test in Sandbox_Map</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, map launcher top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene and builds a new map. Alt + mouse wheel zooms the camera out.
Body colors: you blue, allies cyan, citizens pale yellow, neutral gray; enemies red (goons), orange (Champions)
and purple (Leaders). Labels read "Team: entity", such as "Enemy: Grey". 8a's map tests still hold.

<b>Fog of war</b>
1. On Play the map is black except what you can see from the crossing: the streets around you, up to 100 m,
   stopping at walls. Building interiors you can't see into stay black.
2. Walk along a street: what you pass becomes dimmed (explored) behind you, and stays dimmed. Black never comes
   back to a place you've seen.
3. Stand outside a window: you see into the building through it. Walk up to a door: it opens and you see in.
   Break a wall: you see through the gap.
4. Build a new map (Build or New seed): the whole new map starts black again.
5. "Fog: off" shows the whole map and everything on it; "Fog: on" brings back the fog with what you'd explored.

<b>Hiding</b>
6. Spawn Grey, then step round a corner so a wall is between you: the Grey, its label and its bars vanish, and
   come back when it's in sight again. In dimmed areas you see the buildings but not the Grey.
7. Stand in a doorway with the inside dimmed: you and your label never hide. (Loose Valuables hide the same way
   as the Grey, but this map has none until Milestone 9 fills sockets.)

<b>Playtest colors</b>
8. Build the Repo Agency sandbox again (Zenthium > Setup > Create Repo Agency Sandbox Scene): GleebPeep is purple;
   Tractor Drone, Shield Bearer, Auditor and Notary orange; Greys and the Tow Saucer red; citizens pale yellow.
   A goon that telegraphs still flashes red and goes back to its color after.
