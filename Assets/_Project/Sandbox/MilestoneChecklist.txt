<b>Milestone 7b: what to test in Sandbox_Repo</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, hero select top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene. The lost Valuables count is at the top center.
The Repo squad (GleebPeep, 2 Greys, Tractor Drone, Shield Bearer, Auditor, Notary) patrols the north, all at rank
B. A Tow Saucer waits by the prop row south of you. Green discs are crop circles (east and west). The red button
switches the Mothership Strike on and off. 7a's behavior (dragging citizens, crop circles, GleebPeep) still holds.

<b>Tow Saucer</b>
1. At the start, the Tow Saucer beams the bench (the only prop light enough) and tows it into a crop circle,
   where it vanishes. It then tows or beams up anything else in 15 m: a citizen is abducted in the circle, a
   Valuable goes into its bag count and is lost in the circle.
2. Hit the Tow Saucer while it tows: it lets go where it is.
3. Defeat the Tow Saucer next to a goon and a citizen: it crashes, hurting everyone within 5 m (you, its own
   side and citizens too), for 50 before Armor.

<b>Auditor and Notary</b>
4. Let the Auditor see you from far off while the rest of the squad hasn't: every squad member within 30 m gets
   the "!" at once and comes for you, even behind cover.
5. Next to the Notary, squad members' labels show Notarized, and they take 25% less damage. Pull one more than 10 m from the Notary: it loses Notarized within half a second. The Notary itself
   never has it.

<b>Tractor Beam props and the Mothership</b>
6. Stand behind a prop in line with GleebPeep: Tractor Beam pulls the prop toward it too. It doesn't break
   (the bench too) and hurts no one on the way.
7. Press "Mothership: off": it turns on and counts down 30 s. Then a red ring with a 5 s countdown appears where
   you stand; anyone still inside when it lands takes 300 Energy damage before defenses. It repeats every 30 s
   until you switch it off.
