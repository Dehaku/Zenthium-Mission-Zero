<b>Milestone 4a: what to test</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. Buttons are on the HUD, bottom left.)

<b>Setup</b> (hero select, top right): Rush Hour, Unlocked slots 4, Apply.

<b>Actives</b>
1. Power 1, Speed Punch: a dummy takes 45 (50 minus its 5 Armor), no knockback, 1 s cooldown.
2. Power 4, Uppercut: same damage, the dummy flies 15 m, costs 15 Stamina.
3. Power 2, Dash: 8 m along your aim, stops at walls; turret shots can't hurt you mid-dash.
4. Power 3, Time Slow: 10 s of half speed for dummies, turret and shots, not for you. 30 s cooldown.
5. Time Stop: pick it as the test module, Apply, use Power 4. Shots freeze in the air and you can walk
   through them; frozen dummies still take Kick damage and fly when time resumes.

<b>Passives</b>
6. Sprinter: Sprint is +100% instead of +50%; Stamina 140 with +11/s regen (HUD).
7. Weather the Storm: set Unlocked slots 2 (Padded Suit's 30 Armor would hide the damage), Apply.
   Stand next to the turret (top right corner) so it Kicks you: each hit costs 5 Stamina, does 19 instead
   of 25, and doesn't push you. With Unlocked slots 1 (no Weather the Storm), its Kick pushes you 4 m.
8. Express Delivery: drag the defeated civilian (bottom left corner) and one defeated dummy at once.
9. Charming: the three dummies start within 8 m of you, so they're Charmed at once. Walk up to the
   turret: it becomes Charmed when you get within 8 m. Each goon rolls once (5% convert), but conversion
   does nothing visible until Milestone 6, so only the Charmed mark shows.

<b>Gear</b>
10. Gear 1, First Aid Spray: heals 25% max HP, 20 s cooldown. Next to the downed civilian: revives it, free.
11. Gear 2, Evac Balloon: next to a downed dummy or civilian, it grows as it rises toward the camera,
    then vanishes.
12. Gear 3, Padded Suit: Armor +30, so turret shots do 1 (the 5% minimum) instead of 10 with Unlocked slots 1.
13. Gear 4, Megaphone: dummies within 10 m are Stunned 3 s (label), or resist about half the time.

<b>Other</b>
14. Unlocked slots 1, Apply: only Speed Punch, Sprinter and First Aid Spray are usable.
15. The turret fires every 2 s wherever you are; each shot flies 15 m (Energy Blaster's range), half the
    room, then disappears. Shots never push you: Energy Blaster has no knockback.
16. Major Overclock and Rune: most modules say "not built yet". That's Milestone 4b.
