<b>Milestone 6a: what to test in Sandbox_AI</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, hero select top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene. "Enemy FF" (above it) is the enemy friendly fire dev toggle.
Squad A (3 melee goons, Speed Punch) patrols north-east; Squad B (2 Energy Blaster goons and a Cannons goon)
patrols north. Cover hides them and the Heavy Turret (south-west) from your start. Each goon's AI state is under it.
One more goon is locked in the Stone shop's back room (its locked door is Wood here).

<b>Noticing</b>
1. Watch in the Scene view: each squad walks between its 3 sockets at half speed and waits ~3 s at each (Idle).
2. Unaware goons notice you only within 15 m: about 3 s at 10 m, faster closer, at once within 1 m. They see
   all around. Step out within 15 m: the yellow bar fills; duck behind cover before it's full and it drains
   (3 s from full). Once aware, they see you out to 100 m.
3. Full bar: a "!" pops up and a grey 10 m circle shows; the goon stands still for 0.5 s, then acts. Unaware
   squadmates inside the circle get their own "!" and circle (alerts chain).
4. Sneak up on an unaware goon (Time Slow slows its bar too) and Kick it before its bar fills: it notices at once.

<b>Fighting</b>
5. Melee goons chase you and Speed Punch. Ranged goons stop at range and shoot; walk within 3 m of one and it
   backs off. Labels read "Combat > Player".
6. The Cannons goon turns red for 0.5 s before it fires, aimed where you stood when it turned red: sidestep it.
7. Ranged shots that hit other goons hurt them, but goons never turn on each other. Click "Enemy FF" (off): no
   more damage between goons.
8. Hide behind a building: they go to where they last saw you, wander there ~10 s (Searching), then the bar
   drains for 3 s and they go back to Idle and their patrol.

<b>World</b>
9. Stand outside the Stone shop's west window (don't break it), within 15 m of the goon inside: it notices you
   through the glass, walks to the locked door, punches it until it breaks (Wood, 100 HP), and comes for you.
10. Test module Fire Wand: set the Wood house on fire. Goons path around it, and one inside walks out.
11. The turret notices and shoots you and goons it can see, never the civilian beside it. Goons in Combat with
    no one better in view attack the civilian.

<b>Hero modules</b> (hero select)
12. Major Overclock: Taunt goons in view, unaware ones too: they all go for you.
13. Rune: Smoke Bomb (Gear) between you and goons: they can't see you through it, and unaware goons inside it
    lose their bars.
14. Rune: Ink Beast follows you, staying within ~10 m outside combat, and fights goons it notices.
