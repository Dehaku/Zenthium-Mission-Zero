<b>Milestone 8a: what to test in Sandbox_Map</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. HUD bottom left, map launcher top right.)
Reset scene (bottom right) reloads the scene and builds a new map. Alt + mouse wheel zooms the camera out.
Lot colors show the district: gray Downtown (Stone walls), brown Industrial (Metal), green Residential (Wood).
Blue squares are exit zones, green discs crop circles. Each building's name floats above it.

<b>Building maps</b>
1. On Play, a Medium map builds around a Bank, with you on the street crossing nearest its center. The launcher
   reads 3 x 3 blocks, 252 m, key building Bank. The Console has one "Map:" line and no errors.
2. Pick Small and press Build: 2 x 2 blocks, 172 m. Pick Large: 5 x 5 blocks, 412 m. Build each a few times
   (New seed): blocks never overlap, every block has streets on all four sides, and blocks appear turned.
3. Pick Lab: every block is Industrial (brown). Pick House: every block is Residential (green). Pick Park: the
   key building reads "none (outdoor)" and the map has a pond-and-trees block.
4. Note the seed, press Build again: the same map. Press New seed: a different one.
5. Pick SWIFT Tower and Build: the launcher says no Downtown block holds it, and the old map stays.

<b>Exits, crop circles and the NavMesh</b>
6. The exit count sets how many blue squares appear, spread round the outer street on the map's edges.
7. With Crop circles ticked, rank D gives 1 green disc, B gives 3 and S gives 5, each on open ground in a block.
   Untick it: none.
8. Press Spawn Grey: a Grey appears on the open ground nearest you (one per press, each in a new spot). Once it
   notices you, it walks to you along streets and through doorways, round ponds and trees, never through walls.
9. Break a building wall, then stand inside: a Grey now paths through the gap.
10. Build a Large map and note the NavMesh bake time in the launcher. Tell Claude the Medium and Large times.
