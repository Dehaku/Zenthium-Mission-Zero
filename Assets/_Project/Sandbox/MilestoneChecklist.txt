<b>Milestone 4b: what to test</b>   (Caps Lock hides this. Buttons are on the HUD, bottom left.)
Hero select (top right): pick the hero, Unlocked slots 4, Apply. Dummies have 100 HP and 5 Armor.
The debug panel (top left) now shows and hits <b>you</b>: untick "From attacker" to hit yourself.

<b>Major Overclock</b> (Charge starts full, so Overclocked's +50% is on until Charge drops below 196)
1. Power 1, Taunt: dummies in sight show Taunted and Marked for 4 s (about half resist). While it lasts,
   the turret is knocked back 4 m each time its Kick hits you (stand next to it, top right corner).
   Damage cut: set Unlocked slots 1, Apply (Armor 10). Panel Hit 100: 90 normally, 45 while taunting.
2. Power 2, Shockwave Stomp: dummies within 6 m take 85 (55 without Overclocked) and fly 8 m.
3. Power 3, Power Throw: drag a downed dummy (F), then Power 3: it flies 15 m along your aim.
4. Power 4, Energy Shield: a blue bar in front of your aim for 3 s (30 Charge). Turret shots stop at it;
   dummies it touches are pushed 4 m.
5. Passives: Charge 245 (+6/s) on the HUD. The turret's Kick never pushes you (Immovable). Each hit adds 5
   Armor, up to 50 (label: ReactivePlating; panel: Armor).
6. Gear 1, Repulsor Blast: a yellow beam along your aim (15 Charge); the first dummy within 15 m takes 70
   (45 without Overclocked) and flies 4 m. A wall stops the beam.
7. Gear 3, Breaching Charge: a charge 2 m ahead, a red circle 15 m wide and a 5 s countdown; then every
   dummy in it is defeated (300) and flies 8 m. It never hurts you. Time Stop (test module) holds the countdown.
8. Gear 4, Stasis Cuffs: next to a downed dummy, it's captured where it lies (label: Captured). After that
   nothing touches it: Kicks don't move it, Drag (F) skips it, and Shockwave Stomp doesn't move it either.

<b>Rune</b>
9. Power 1, Ink Beast: 2 beasts (Living Ink) for 20 s. They chase dummies within 15 m and lash them for 35
   each (25 from your Will 20, +15 Force Spell Tome, minus 5 Armor); with no dummy near, they follow you.
10. Power 2, Glyph Trap: a purple disc at the mouse, up to 15 m. Place it under a dummy: dummies within 3 m
    are Rooted 3 s (a third resist), then it's gone. A new cast replaces an untriggered one.
11. Power 4, Shockwave Stomp: 95 to dummies within 6 m (Strength 20).
12. Deathly Rage and Second Wind: panel Hit 10000 with "From attacker" off. Defiance procs (102%); you get
    Deathly Rage (label; panel: Strength 60, max HP 300), and Second Wind heals you to 300.
13. Fresh Ink: defeating a dummy with your own hit heals 5 HP.
14. Gear 3, Smoke Bomb: a grey cloud at the mouse, up to 8 m, for 5 s. Dummies inside are Stunned while in
    it (about half resist); you aren't.
15. Hold MoreInfo: every module of both heroes is listed, none "not built yet".
